# CouchCape credits & data sources

- Star positions, magnitudes and colour indices (`data/stars.bin`, `data/starnames.json`): **HYG Database** by David Nash (astronexus), licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). See `data/HYG-LICENSE.txt`. The binary packing (RA, Dec, mag, B-V as float32) was made for the owner's game Attack of the AI and is redistributed here under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.
- Deep-sky object list (`data/dso.json`, Messier/NGC positions, types, magnitudes, sizes): carried over from Attack of the AI alongside the HYG data; treat it as CC BY-SA 4.0 like the star data.
- Engine: three.js r160 (MIT).
- Everything else (models, textures, shaders, sky, sounds) is original and generated in code.
