Hand Signs

Your arms never have to hold a pose. Rest your hands on your armrests or lap, palms down, and use these small signs. Either hand works.

Resting signs (arms down)

FlySqueeze one hand into a fist and hold. Let go to slow down.
BoostSqueeze both fists.
SteerLook where you want to go. Look up to climb, down to dive.
Use your powerLook at a target, then touch thumb to pointer finger.
Open the mapTouch thumb to middle finger. Look at a place and pinch to go.
Change viewTouch thumb to ring finger: chase view, over the shoulder, first person.

Big signs (optional)

Hero flightReach an open hand out in front of you.
GlideRaise both arms high and wide once. You keep flying with arms down. Repeat to stop.
MapHold an open palm in front of your face for half a second.
Say hiWave at a Glimmerling.

No hands at all

Hands-freeTurn on Hands-free mode in Settings. Look at something and hold your gaze to choose it. Powers fire on their own when you look at a foe.

Tip: keep your hands where the headset can see them, on the armrests or your lap rather than under a blanket.
