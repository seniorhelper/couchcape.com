CouchCape Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version: CouchCape has no accounts and no ads, and we never sell your data. Your settings and progress stay on your own device.

Who we are

CouchCape is published by Eye To Ad Media, 1001 Bannock St #660, Denver, CO 80204, USA. Questions? Email (enable JavaScript to see our email).

What stays on your device

We store your game settings (comfort, difficulty, narration and accessibility options) and your progress in your browser's local storage on your headset or computer. This data is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Hand tracking, head tracking and eye/head aiming

Your headset's browser processes hand and head tracking on the device so you can play without controllers. CouchCape does not record or store this tracking data. In multiplayer, only the position and rotation needed to show your hero to other players are shared with the players in your session, and only while you play.

Multiplayer

When you join a shared session, other players in that session receive: a random session ID (not your name or account), your chosen hero, and your hero's position. You can mute, block or report any player from the in-game Players menu. Reports are reviewed under our community rules and may be used to remove players who break them.

Voice

The spoken guide uses your device's built-in speech voice; no audio leaves your device. This version of CouchCape does not use your microphone. If voice chat is added later, it will ask permission first, default to push-to-talk, and we will update this policy before it goes live.

What we don't do

No advertising, no ad tracking, no selling or renting personal data, no collecting names, emails, location or contacts inside the game.

Children

CouchCape is rated for ages 13 and up and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights and deletion

Because game data lives on your device, you control it: clear the site's data to delete it. For any data related to a report or a support request, email us and we will delete it within 30 days, as required by laws such as the GDPR and CCPA.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for meaningful changes, note it in the game.
